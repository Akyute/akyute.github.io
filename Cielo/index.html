Cielo
Generative A/V


2021 Cielo { Proyecto Audire, Braga }

( ENG )
See below for Spanish

Locations
2022: Proyecto Audire, Braga, PT

Cielo (from latín caelum; from caelum tangi: being – touched – hurt from the ray) is an generative A/V installation that gives sound to the colour spectrum created from a series of artificial sunsets made using AI GAN.  

The various nuances of colour are analyzed and translated into sound, in order to create a synaethetic experience. 

A performative piece is generated from the installation, as the animation of the graphics, and therefore the sound, are influenced by the users position within the space the visuals are projected into.

The relation between the colours analyzed and the sound designed were founded upon sensory perception, rather than mathematical structure ( c = f λ ), creating a link between the audio and the visual based on a personal nature of the correlations, inspired by the colour theory established by Albert Munsell in 1905 and the emotional wheel by Robert Plutchik in 1980.

The piece was developed in collaboration with Nicolás Dell'Acqua.



( ES )

Cielo (del latín caelum; de caelum tangi: ser –tocado– herido por el rayo) es una A/V instalación generativa que sonoriza el espectro de colores visibles en una serie de atardeceres artificiales generados utilizando AI GAN.  

Los diversos matices de color se analizan y son traducidos a sonido para crear una experiencia sinestésica.

La relación entre los colores analizados y el sonido diseñado se basó en la percepción sensorial, en vez de la estructura matemática ( c = f λ ), creando un vínculo entre el audio y el visual basado en una naturaleza personal de las correlaciones, inspirado en la teoría del color establecida por Albert Munsell en 1905 y la rueda emocional por Robert Plutchik en 1980.

La pieza fue desarrollada en colaboración con Nicolás Dell'Acqua.
